About Us

Your trusted partner for transparent car dealership reviews.

Who We Are

Welcome to Car Dealership Review, a full-stack Django and React application built to help drivers make confident vehicle-buying decisions. Our platform brings together dealership information, vehicle makes and models, verified customer reviews, and sentiment analysis in one easy-to-use experience.

We connect customers with trusted dealerships across the country. Whether you are searching for a family SUV in Kansas or a performance car in California, our dealership review community gives you the details you need before you visit the showroom.
